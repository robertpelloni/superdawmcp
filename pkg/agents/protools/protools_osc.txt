Pro Tools OSC Mapping (Conceptual for SuperDAW-MCP)
--------------------------------------------------
/protools/transport/play [int: 0/1]
/protools/transport/stop [int: 1]
/protools/track/volume [string: track_id, float: 0.0-1.0]
/protools/track/pan [string: track_id, float: -1.0-1.0]
/protools/track/create [string: name, string: type]

Note: This mapping should be loaded into a Pro Tools compatible OSC bridge like 'OSC/PILOT' or 'DAW Remote'.
